⚠️ Reminder: Active Participation Required

Dear Students,

This is an important reminder for all students who have registered for the AI Skills Lab.

We have observed two categories of registered students:

1. Students who attended initially but are no longer attending
Some students reported to the lab during the initial period but have not been visiting or participating in recent lab activities.
2. Students who registered but have not reported to the lab
Some students completed the registration process but have not yet visited or reported to the AI Skills Lab.

The AI Skills Lab is intended to provide students with opportunities for:

🚀   Hands-on AI learning
💡   AI project development and innovation
🔬   Research and technical activities
🏆   Hackathons and technical competitions
🎓   Workshops, training and skill-development programs
🤝   Industry interaction and collaborative activities
📌 Action Required
If you have registered for the AI Skills Lab and are genuinely interested in continuing, you are requested to start reporting to the lab and actively participate in the ongoing activities.

Students who are unable to attend due to a genuine reason are requested to communicate the same to the AI Skills Lab Coordinators.

Please Note:
Continued registration without active participation may result in the student being removed from the active membership list, so that opportunities can be provided to other interested students.

We encourage all registered students to take advantage of the opportunities available through the AI Skills Lab and become actively involved in its activities.

We look forward to your active participation.
